Darkens the picture below the TV's own lowest brightness. It stays on when the marker is hidden and after the TV restarts. Emergency off: hold the overlay button on the remote (Info, unless you chose another one) for about one second.

Limits: it darkens the picture, not the backlight, so blacks don't get blacker. The volume bar, keyboard and the TV's own picture menus can show above it, and some protected or HDR video may not dim.
